## 2. Conditions of Carriage
When a passenger buys an Emirates ticket, it constitutes a contract of carriage. This contract allows the passenger to travel on one flight or a series of flights.

The terms of the contract are governed by:
1. The terms and conditions of the ticket.
2. The Conditions of Carriage.
3. Applicable tariffs.
4. Emirates’ regulations.

Reference: Emirates provides a full Conditions of Carriage document as a PDF.

## 3. Worldwide Notices

### 3.1 Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking
Modern slavery, servitude, forced labour, and human trafficking are crimes and violations of fundamental human rights.
The UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 addresses slavery in the United Kingdom and consolidates previous offences relating to trafficking and slavery.
The Emirates Group states that it complies with the laws of the countries in which it operates, including laws concerning anti-human trafficking and anti-slavery. It maintains a zero-tolerance approach to modern slavery and commits to acting ethically and with integrity in its business dealings, relationships, and supply chains.

Related documents listed by Emirates:
- Emirates Group Anti-Slavery and Human Trafficking Policy
- Emirates Statement on Modern Slavery

### 3.2 Website Accessibility
Emirates states that it aims to make its website accessible to the broadest possible audience, regardless of technology or physical capability.
The supplied notice refers to improving website usability in accordance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA Success Criteria.

## 4. Argentina Notices

### 4.1 Complaint Procedure for Emirates Flights to or from Argentina
The supplied notice states that this procedure is issued under Resolution 774/2025 of Argentina’s National Civil Aviation Administration (ANAC) and applies to Emirates flights to or from Argentina.

#### How to File a Complaint
- **Online:** Submit a complaint using the Emirates feedback or complaints form. An email confirming submission is sent immediately, followed by an acknowledgment email containing a complaint reference number.
- **By telephone:** Call Emirates Customer Affairs at +971 4 708 3611.
  - Monday to Thursday: 7:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. UAE local time.
  - Friday: 7:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. UAE local time.
- **By email:** customer.affairs@emirates.com

#### Information Required
A complaint must include at least:
- Flight details.
- Booking reference and ticket number.
- Full name.
- National identity document or passport number.
- Telephone number and email address.
- A clear description of the claim.

#### Processing Time and Escalation
Complaints are stated to be processed within 30 working days after all required information has been received. If the response is delayed or the passenger is dissatisfied with the outcome, the complaint may be escalated to ANAC through its website.

## 5. Canada Notices

### 5.1 Advance Passenger Information and Passenger Name Records (API/PNR)
The Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) is authorized to collect advance arrival information, advance departure information, and exit records under the legislation referenced in the supplied notice.
The information may be used to:
- Identify individuals who may pose a threat to Canada’s safety or security.
- Support investigation, questioning, or examination of arriving travellers.
- Verify before departure that incoming air travellers have the required documents to enter Canada or qualify for an exemption.
- Identify high-risk people and goods departing or intending to depart Canada.

CBSA entry and exit records may be shared with other government departments for immigration, social benefits, or law-enforcement purposes.

#### Passenger Rights Regarding Information
According to the notice, travellers may submit a written request to the CBSA to:
- Obtain a copy of their prescribed entry and exit information.
- Request correction of information they believe is incorrect.
- Complain about an unfair refusal to provide access, failure to correct information, or other incorrect handling of their information.

The notice refers travellers to the CBSA’s API/PNR, IAPI, and Air Exit programs information.

### 5.2 Primary Inspection Kiosks and ArriveCAN
The supplied notice describes the introduction of Primary Inspection Kiosks at Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ), replacing the manual customs declaration form.
It also states that travellers on Emirates flights to Canada can submit their customs and immigration declaration in advance using the ArriveCAN app within 72 hours before departure to help avoid delays on arrival.
Travellers are directed to the Canada Border Services Agency website for further information.

*Currency note:* The kiosk implementation date and ArriveCAN procedures should be checked against current Canadian government guidance before being used for live travel advice.

### 5.3 Canadian Air Passenger Protection Regulations (APPR)
The notice directs passengers to Emirates’ applicable tariff for the full terms and conditions governing carriage to and from Canada.
Additional notices listed include:
- Flight Delay, Tarmac Delay and Flight Cancellation Notice.
- Denied Boarding Notice.

Passengers with questions about their rights under the Canadian Air Passenger Protection Regulations, or who wish to request compensation, are advised to contact Emirates Customer Affairs through the feedback or complaints form. They may also contact the Canadian Transportation Agency.

## 7. Colombia Notices

### 7.1 Right of Withdrawal
For tickets purchased in Colombia, the supplied notice states that passengers may cancel their trip up to 24 hours before the scheduled departure of their first flight and receive a refund.
Emirates may retain a fee of up to 10% of the fare, excluding fees, taxes, and administrative fees.
This right does not apply to promotional fares.

### 7.2 Right of Retraction
When a ticket is purchased through the Emirates website or customer contact centre, the passenger may cancel the reservation within five business days of the purchase date and request a refund.
This right cannot be exercised if the first flight in the itinerary departs within five days of the purchase date.

### 7.3 Refund Processing
The notice states that refunds will be made within a maximum of 15 calendar days from the date of the request.
Passengers may submit a refund request using Emirates’ online refund request form.

### 7.4 Colombian Aviation Regulations — RAC 3
Colombia’s Reglamentos Aeronáuticos de Colombia, Actividades Aéreas Civiles (RAC 3) addresses passenger rights relating to:
- Denied boarding.
- Flight delays.
- Flight cancellations.
- Flights brought forward.

The complete regulation is available through Colombia’s Civil Aviation Authority. The notice also refers passengers to the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce and the Civil Aviation Authority for further information.

## 8. European Union Notices

### 8.1 Flight Disruption Rights
The supplied notice states that EC Regulation 261/2004 gives passengers boarding a flight in an EU country certain rights in cases of:
- Denied boarding.
- Flight cancellation.
- Long flight delays.

Emirates lists separate notices covering denied boarding, delays, and cancellations.
Passengers who believe they are entitled to compensation are advised to contact their nearest Emirates office. The notice also refers to National Enforcement Bodies, noting that not all EC countries have appointed one.

### 8.2 Import Restrictions on Animal-Origin Products
The supplied notice refers to EU restrictions on the personal import of products of animal origin, introduced from 1 May 2009. Passengers are directed to the European Commission for further information.

### 8.3 EU Summary of Liability
Emirates provides a Summary of Liability explaining its liabilities toward passengers and their baggage under the referenced EC regulations.

### 8.4 EU Air Safety List
The EU Air Safety List identifies airlines subject to an EU operating ban or restrictions.
The supplied notice states that if Emirates has made a reservation for a passenger on a flight due to be operated by an airline on that list, the passenger may be entitled to reimbursement or rerouting under Article 8 of EC Regulation 261/2004, subject to the regulation’s eligibility requirements.

### 8.5 Insolvency Protection
The supplied notice states that Emirates has insolvency protection with International Passenger Protection Limited (IPP), acting as Cover Holder for Certain Underwriters at Lloyd’s.
The stated insurance applies to money paid to Emirates for qualifying Linked Travel Arrangements and Package Travel Arrangements, as defined under the referenced UK regulations and EU Directive 2015/2302.

For an insolvency-related claim, the notice provides:
- Telephone: +44 (0)345 266 1872
- Claims website: www.ipplondon.co.uk/claims.asp

*Currency note:* Legal references, insurance arrangements, and passenger eligibility may change. Check the current Emirates and relevant government notices before giving legal or compensation advice.

## 9. Hong Kong, China Notices

### 9.1 Ban on Alternative Smoking Products
The supplied notice states that, from 30 April 2022, Hong Kong prohibits the possession and importation of alternative smoking products in parcels, packages, or cargo.
The listed products include:
- Electronic smoking products.
- Heated tobacco products.
- Herbal cigarettes.
- Parts and accessories associated with these products.

The notice states that incoming travellers are not allowed to carry these items as baggage.

#### Exceptions Described in the Source
- Medicines approved under the Pharmacy and Poisons Ordinance.
- Transit passengers connecting through Hong Kong International Airport without passing through immigration.

The source states a maximum penalty of six months’ imprisonment and a fine of USD 50,000 for non-compliance.
*Currency note:* This is a date-specific legal notice supplied by the source. Verify current Hong Kong law and penalties before advising travellers.

## 10. India Notices

### 10.1 Refund Request Within 48 Hours of Ticket Issuance
The supplied notice refers to Civil Aviation Requirements — Section 3, Series M, Part II.
It states that a passenger can request a full refund for their itinerary within 48 hours after ticket issuance if both conditions are met:
1. The itinerary starts in India.
2. The booking was made on Emirates.com more than 15 days before the first departure flight.

Both conditions must be satisfied under the wording of the supplied notice.
*Currency note:* The applicable regulation, eligibility conditions, and current refund procedure should be verified before providing a definitive answer about a passenger’s entitlement.

## 11. Israel Notices

### 11.1 Aviation Services Law
The notice refers to Israel’s Aviation Services Law (Compensation and Assistance for Flight Cancellations or Changes of Conditions), 5772–2012.
It directs passengers to information about their rights in cases of:
- Denied boarding.
- Flight delays.
- Flight cancellations.
- Flights brought forward.

### 11.2 Consumer Protection Law
The notice refers to Israel’s Consumer Protection Law, 5741–1981, concerning ticket cancellations.
Passengers are directed to the relevant legal information for further details about their rights.

### 11.3 Passenger Data Transfer
The supplied notice states that Emirates is required by Israeli law to submit information collected during the ticket reservation process to Israel’s National Center for Passenger Data Analysis.
According to the notice, authorities may use the data for purposes including:
- Combating terrorism.
- Securing air travel.
- Combating serious crime.
- Improving border control.
- Protecting public health.

The data is retained for the periods established by law. The notice directs passengers to the Center’s website for additional information, data-protection details, and the full text of the law.

## 13. Maldives Notices

Eligible customers may visit the Emirates ticket office in Malé to request a refund based on the applicable taxes for their journey type.

## 17. Tanzania Notices

### 17.1 Plastic Carrier Bag Restrictions
The supplied notice states that all plastic carrier bags, regardless of thickness, are prohibited from being imported, exported, manufactured, sold, stored, supplied, or used in mainland Tanzania.
Travellers going to Tanzania are advised not to carry or pack plastic bags in checked baggage or hand luggage.

#### Exceptions Described in the Source
- Plastic packaging for medical purposes.
- Plastic packaging for foodstuffs.
- Plastic packaging for sanitary purposes.
- Plastic packaging for waste-management services.
- Resealable “Ziploc” bags used specifically for carrying toiletries, which are expected to remain in the traveller’s possession throughout the visit.

## 20. United States of America Notices

### 20.1 Advance Passenger Information — Privacy Notice
The supplied notice reproduces a Transportation Security Administration (TSA) privacy notice applicable to passengers travelling to or from the United States.
It states that the TSA requires the following information for watch-list screening:
- Full name.
- Date of birth.
- Gender.
A Redress Number may also be provided if available.
Passengers are directed to the TSA website for its privacy policies: www.tsa.gov

### 20.2 Emirates Tarmac Delay Contingency Plan
The plan applies to international scheduled and public charter flights operated by Emirates to and from the USA when irregular operations result in a lengthy tarmac delay at a US airport.
Possible causes include weather events, air traffic control system issues, government operating restrictions, and airport construction projects. Emirates will implement its listed measures regardless of the cause.

#### Opportunity to Leave the Aircraft
Passengers must be given an opportunity to deplane before a tarmac delay exceeds four hours on covered international flights, unless:
1. The pilot-in-command determines that a safety-related or security-related reason prevents the aircraft from leaving its position to deplane passengers; or
2. Air traffic control advises that returning to the gate or another disembarkation point would significantly disrupt airport operations.
The general US rule requires an opportunity to deplane before three hours for domestic flights or four hours for international flights.

#### Food, Water, Lavatories, and Medical Attention
The plan states that Emirates will:
- Provide adequate food and potable water no later than two hours after the start of a tarmac delay, unless safety or security considerations prevent it.
- Provide operable lavatory facilities while the aircraft remains on the tarmac.
- Provide adequate medical attention when needed.

#### Passenger Updates
For covered tarmac delays exceeding 30 minutes at a US airport, Emirates will:
- Provide an update when the delay exceeds 30 minutes.
- Provide further updates as appropriate.
- Notify passengers promptly whenever a suitable opportunity to disembark becomes available.

### 20.3 US Customer Service Plan
Emirates states that its US Customer Service Plan was developed in accordance with US Department of Transportation regulations, 14 CFR 259.5.

## 22. General Guidance for Passenger Rights Queries
When answering passenger-rights questions using this document:
1. Identify the country or jurisdiction relevant to the passenger’s journey.
2. Identify the issue, such as cancellation, delay, denied boarding, refund, data privacy, or customs restrictions.
3. Use the applicable notice rather than assuming that the same rules apply worldwide.
4. Distinguish between information explicitly stated in the supplied notice and details that the notice does not provide.
5. Direct passengers to the relevant Emirates department or government authority when the source recommends doing so.
6. Treat dates, fees, legal references, and regulatory requirements as potentially subject to change.
7. Do not promise compensation or a refund solely because a notice mentions passenger rights; eligibility may depend on the applicable law and individual circumstances.
