# Emirates — Check-in Options, Group Travel and Alternative Check-in Locations

## 1. Online, Mobile and Airport Check-in

### Online Check-in
- Online check-in is available up to 48 hours before the flight.
- Passengers can select their seat and dietary meal during online check-in.
- Passengers can drop off their baggage at the airport after checking in online.
- Check-in portal: https://www.emirates.com/in/english/manage-booking/online-check-in/

### Check-in Using the Emirates App
- Passengers can check in through the Emirates App.
- Boarding passes can be downloaded and stored on the app for use at nearly all airports.
- Passenger details can be saved in the app to make future check-ins quicker.
- App information: https://www.emirates.com/in/english/book/about-booking-online/emirates-app/

### Airport Check-in
- Emirates provides check-in desks arranged by travel class or Emirates Skywards membership tier.
- Staff assist passengers with baggage check-in.
- A mobile boarding pass can be sent to the passenger's email.
- At most airports, check-in opens three hours before departure. At some airports, it may open up to four hours before departure. Passengers should check their ticket for the recommended arrival time.

### Passport Control, Security and Boarding Gate Deadlines
| Requirement | Deadline before departure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Complete passport control and security | 90 minutes |
| Reach the gate — Premium Economy and Economy | 60 minutes |
| Reach the gate — First and Business Class | 45 minutes |

#### Estimated walking times to gates at Dubai International Airport (DXB):
- **A gates:** up to 20 minutes.
- **B gates:** up to 15 minutes.
- **C gates:** up to 25 minutes.

*Note:* These are source-provided timings and should be checked against current airport guidance before being treated as current policy.

### Self Check-in Kiosks
- Passengers can use self-service kiosks to check in, tag their baggage and collect their boarding passes.
- Baggage can be dropped off at designated bag-drop kiosks.
- Some kiosks support contactless operation through a mobile phone.
- Contactless journey information: https://www.emirates.com/in/english/experience/contactless-journey/

### Emirates Check-in Ports
- Portable check-in stations are available at the airport, where staff can assist passengers.
- Passengers can receive a mobile boarding pass by email or request a printed boarding pass.
- Baggage can be tagged at the station, and porters take it to the flight.

## 2. Group Travel Services

### Eligibility and Booking Requirements
- Emirates Group Travel Services apply to groups of 10 or more passengers.
- Passengers must travel together on the same flight and itinerary.
- Bookings should be arranged through a local Emirates office or travel agent.
- Availability depends on the departure and arrival airports.
- Local Emirates office information: https://www.emirates.com/in/english/help/offices/

### Advance Planning Deadlines
| Requirement | Notice period stated in the source |
| :--- | :--- |
| Individual meal requirements | At least 24 hours before departure; preferably at booking |
| Group seating arrangements | At least 7 days before departure |
| Personalised menus and headrest covers | At least 15 days before departure |
| Cabin crew language requirements | 30 days in advance |

### Group Check-in at Dubai International Airport
- Separate group check-in desks are available for eligible groups, including school trips.
- Groups are advised to arrive three hours before departure.
- The supplied source directs groups to check-in zone C to check in together.

### Group Travel Benefits
- **At the airport:** Pre-arranged advance check-in; Dedicated group check-in desks, where available; Group-labelled baggage tags for easier identification; Assistance arranging UAE visas for group members who require them.
- **On board:** Group seating arrangements; Personalised menus and headrest covers displaying an organisation's logo; Welcome announcements for the group; Cabin crew who speak the group's required language (subject to advance arrangements); Pre-paid celebratory beverages and chocolates on most routes; Meals catering to special religious or dietary requirements.

### Types of Group Travel
- Hajj and Umrah groups (https://www.emirates.com/english/experience/seasonal-occasions/hajj/)
- School trips
- Sports teams
- Corporate travel (https://www.emirates.com/in/english/before-you-fly/corporate-travel/)

## 3. Alternative Check-in Locations and Services

### Emirates City Check-in and Travel Store — DIFC, Dubai
- **Location:** ICD Brookfield Place, Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC).
- Passengers can check in and drop off their baggage away from the airport.
- Baggage can be dropped off from 24 hours until four hours before departure.
- Check-in services are available daily from 08:00 to 22:00.
- Ticketing and reservations are available from 10:00 to 21:30.
- Services include staffed check-in, self-service kiosks and a robot check-in assistant.
- The store also sells Emirates-branded products and offers delivery of purchases.
- Office information: https://www.emirates.com/in/english/help/offices/dxb/dubai/

### Emirates City Check-in — Ajman
- **Location:** Ajman Central Bus Terminal.
- 24-hour city check-in service.
- Passengers can check in from 24 hours until four hours before departure.
- Passengers can present their health documents and check in their baggage.
- A mobile boarding pass can be emailed, or a printed pass can be requested.
- Location: https://maps.app.goo.gl/FPhLT4ZhhBJESM586

### Emirates Cruise Check-in
Emirates provides flight check-in facilities for cruise passengers arriving in Dubai.
- **Locations:** Port Rashid (Cruise Terminal 3); Dubai Harbour (Terminals A and B in Dubai Marina).
- **Services and conditions:**
  - Passengers can check in for their flight after disembarking from their cruise ship.
  - Staff weigh and tag baggage at the port check-in desks.
  - Boarding passes can be emailed as mobile passes or printed.
  - Baggage is transferred directly to the passenger's flight at Dubai International Airport.
  - No additional charge for port check-in and baggage delivery.
  - Check-in available from arrival at the port until four hours before flight departure.
  - The port check-in facilities are wheelchair accessible.
  - Passengers can leave their baggage at the port and explore Dubai before continuing to the airport without returning to collect their bags.

## 4. Additional Home Check-in Eligibility Detail
- Emirates Skywards Platinum members departing from Dubai can receive complimentary Home Check-in.
- Complimentary Home Check-in is available to eligible First Class passengers departing from Dubai (full-fare First Class tickets qualify for complimentary service; tickets purchased with Skywards Miles or upgraded using cash or Miles require the paid service).
- Booking links:
  - Standard Home Check-in: https://marhaba.dubz.com/order/hci?airline=emirates
  - First Class Home Check-in: https://marhaba.dubz.com/order/hci?airline=emirates-first
