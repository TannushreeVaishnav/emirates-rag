# Emirates — Disrupted Travel, Special Travel Advisories and Denied Boarding Vouchers

## 1. Overview
This document covers Emirates' guidance for disrupted flights, rebooking options, severe-weather waivers, Dubai Connect during disruptions, selected travel advisories, and compensation vouchers for denied boarding.

## 2. Disruption Advice

### 2.1 Flight Delay or Cancellation Notifications
If an Emirates flight is delayed or cancelled, Emirates sends an email and SMS notification to the contact details provided in the booking. Passengers should keep contact details updated through Manage Your Booking or inform their travel agent.

### 2.2 Rebooking Disrupted Flights
Emirates first tries to rebook passengers on the first available Emirates connection. If no Emirates flights are available, alternative carrier options will be explored. Contact Emirates if connecting flights or return journeys need adjusting.

### 2.3 Changes to Rebooked Flights or Cancellations
If the rebooked date or time does not suit the passenger, contact Emirates. Changes depend on booked class of travel, flight availability, and options available for the disrupted booking.

### 2.4 Hotel Accommodation During a Long Layover in Dubai
Passengers whose connection time in Dubai is between 10 and 24 hours might be eligible for Dubai Connect. Services can be requested through Manage Your Booking after meeting entry and testing requirements.

### 2.5 Options During Severe Weather Waivers
Under a severe-weather waiver, passengers may:
1. Change their travel date to a date within **seven days (±7 days)** before or after the original travel date.
2. Change their booking to the **closest airport in the same country**.
3. Request a **refund** for their ticket.

### 2.6 Delayed or Damaged Baggage After Flight Disruption
Follow procedures in the Emirates Delayed and Damaged Baggage policy:
- Report missing baggage immediately to obtain a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) reference number.
- Track and trace baggage online.

## 3. Special Travel Advisories

### 3.1 USA-bound Flights from Dubai (DXB) Security Measures
Enhanced security measures apply to USA-bound flights departing DXB:
- Dedicated check-in areas for flights to the USA.
- Security-related questions before check-in.
- Enhanced electronic-device checks at boarding gates.
- Early check-in is permitted only on the day of travel, up to 12 hours before departure. All travelling members must appear in person with luggage and documents.

### 3.2 Hong Kong Alternative Smoking Products Ban
Effective 30 April 2022, Hong Kong prohibits the possession, importation, or carriage of alternative smoking products (electronic cigarettes, heated tobacco products, herbal cigarettes, parts/accessories) in baggage, parcels, or cargo.
- **Exceptions:** Approved medicines and transit passengers connecting through HKG without passing immigration.
- **Penalties:** Up to six months' imprisonment and a fine of USD 50,000 for non-compliance.

## 4. Denied Boarding Options Voucher

### 4.1 Compensation Offered for Denied Boarding
Passengers not accepted on their flight receive:
1. A confirmed seat on the next available flight to their final destination.
2. A voucher redeemable for a **complimentary return ticket** on selected Emirates routes.

### 4.2 Voucher Issuance
The voucher is issued by the Emirates Airport Services office once denied boarding is established.

### 4.3 Voucher Redemption Rules and Restrictions
- **Booking Window:** Booking must take place between **7 and 365 days** after the voucher was issued.
- **Ticket Validity:** The issued ticket is valid for **one year** from its issue date.
- **Validity Extension:** Voucher validity **cannot be extended**.
- **Transferability:** Non-transferable. Issued strictly to the individual passenger; cannot be transferred to family or friends.
- **Cash Value:** Cannot be exchanged for cash.
- **Splitting:** Cannot be split into multiple tickets. Covers exactly one return ticket in one booking.
- **Date or Route Changes:** Eligible for changes within the applicable redemption zone without extra charge if:
  - The ticket has not been used at all.
  - The new route remains within the applicable zone.
  - The ticket is still within its one-year validity period.
- **Chauffeur-Drive:** Available on a compensation ticket when travelling in Business Class.
- **Skywards Miles:**
  - Accrual: Compensation tickets do **not** earn Skywards Miles.
  - Upgrades: Passengers **can** upgrade using Skywards Miles or cash (subject to availability).
- **Infant Tickets:** Infant tickets are **not** applicable to denied boarding vouchers.
