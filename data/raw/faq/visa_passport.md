# Emirates — Visa and Passport Requirements, US Travel Authorisation, Dubai Connect and Dubai International Airport

## 1. Visa and Passport Requirements

### 1.1 Responsibility for Travel Documents
- Passengers are responsible for carrying the proper travel documents for their journey.
- Passengers should check the entry requirements for their destination before travelling.

### 1.2 Checking Visa and Passport Requirements
- Emirates provides access to visa and passport requirement information for upcoming trips via the International Air Transport Association (IATA).
- The information is available in English only.
- Requirements may change at short notice and are intended only as a guide. Passengers should confirm requirements with the relevant authorities for their destination countries.
- Holders of non-standard passports and travel documents may have different entry requirements.

## 2. Travelling to and from the United States

### 2.1 Electronic System for Travel Authorisation (ESTA)
Under the US Visa Waiver Program (VWP), citizens of certain eligible nations can apply for an Electronic System for Travel Authorisation (ESTA). ESTA allows eligible travellers to stay in the United States for up to 90 days without applying for a visa.

### 2.2 How to Get an ESTA
- Eligible travellers must apply for an ESTA before travelling to the United States by air or sea.
- Applications can be submitted online at any time.
- Emirates recommends submitting the application no later than 72 hours before the flight.
- If an ESTA application is denied, the traveller should apply for a non-immigrant visa at a US embassy or consulate before arriving at the airport.
- Official US VWP application: https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/visa-waiver-program

### 2.3 TSA PreCheck — Fast-track Security Screening
Emirates partners with the Transportation Security Administration (TSA) to provide fast-track security screening for eligible travellers departing from the United States.
TSA PreCheck allows eligible passengers to pass through security without removing shoes, laptops, belts or liquids.
- **Fee:** USD 85.
- **Validity:** Five years.
- **Application processing time:** Two to three weeks.
- **Eligibility:** Applicants must meet TSA security regulations.
- **US Airports Listed:** New York (JFK), Houston (IAH), Washington (IAD), San Francisco (SFO), Seattle (SEA), Chicago (ORD), Orlando (MCO), Dallas (DFW), Boston (BOS).
- Official TSA website: https://www.tsa.gov/

## 3. Dubai Connect

### 3.1 What Is Dubai Connect?
Dubai Connect is a service for eligible Emirates passengers who have a long connection between flights in Dubai.
When a suitable shorter connection is unavailable, eligible passengers may receive:
- Complimentary hotel accommodation.
- Applicable meals.
- Complimentary transfers between Dubai International Airport and the hotel.
- A visa to enter the UAE, subject to applicable requirements and immigration approval.

Emirates plans its flights to provide suitable connections wherever possible. Dubai Connect may be available when passengers need to wait longer for their next flight. The service is subject to eligibility, availability and applicable terms.

### 3.2 Hotel Accommodation
- Eligible passengers receive a complimentary hotel stay.
- Applicable meals are included in the hotel booking.
- Passengers may need to show a credit card at hotel check-in to cover additional expenses (minibar, spa treatments, non-covered dining).

### 3.3 Visa Arrangements
A complimentary 48-hour visa is provided as part of the Dubai Connect booking where applicable:
- Nationalities requiring a visa should prearrange it at least 48 hours before arrival.
- Visa issuance on arrival, where applicable, is subject to UAE Immigration approval.
- Dubai Connect Visa Request page: https://www.emirates.com/in/english/before-you-fly/dubai-international-airport/dubai-connect-visa-request/

### 3.4 Transfers Between the Airport and Hotel
- A complimentary coach transports eligible passengers between Dubai International Airport and the hotel.
- Meet the Dubai Connect team at the Dubai Connect desk in Arrivals.

### 3.5 How to Book Dubai Connect Online
1. Book Emirates flights.
2. Log in using passenger surname and booking reference.
3. Open the Manage section and select Dubai Connect.
4. Check whether itinerary qualifies and add the service.
- Manage Booking: https://www.emirates.com/in/english/manage-booking/

### 3.6 Booking Through an Emirates Office or Travel Agent
- Contact the office or travel agent to check eligibility and arrange the service in advance.

### 3.7 Eligibility and Booking Deadline Discrepancy (CRITICAL CONFLICT)
- Passengers under 18 can use Dubai Connect if travelling with another passenger over 18.
- Additional conditions may apply depending on booking date, fare paid, flight selected from origin, and first available flight from transit.
- **Booking Deadline Discrepancy in Official Emirates Documentation:**
  - *Main booking instructions state:* Book at least 12 hours before the incoming flight to Dubai.
  - *Additional conditions state:* Emirates can accept Dubai Connect bookings made at least 24 hours before departure of the inbound flight to Dubai.
  - *Resolution rule:* Both statements must be preserved as an official source contradiction. Do not silently pick one. Advise passengers of the 12h vs 24h variation and suggest booking as early as possible.
- Dubai Connect terms and conditions: https://www.emirates.com/in/english/before-you-fly/dubai-international-airport/dubai-connect/terms-and-conditions/

### 3.8 Official Dubai Travel Information
- Dubai travel advisory: https://www.dubaidet.gov.ae/en/travel-advisory

## 4. Dubai International Airport (DXB)

### 4.1 Airport Overview
- DXB is Emirates' home hub.
- Emirates operates from **Terminal 3**.
- Emirates flights arrive at and depart from **Gates A, B, and C**.

### 4.2 Airport Facilities
- Boutique shopping and retail outlets, restaurants and dining, sleep pods, spa treatments, inter-terminal transfers (bus, train, on foot), hotel shuttles, taxi services, and Dubai Metro connections.
